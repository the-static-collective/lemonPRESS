# Little Free Library intake

This directory is the lemonPRESS house register for book-shaped objects recovered from the Static Collective Little Free Library.

## Core rule

**REGISTRATION != PUBLICATION**

The private source shelf stays private unless a work is deliberately released. A record here gives the work a durable lemonPRESS address without copying private manuscripts, witness material, Google Drive IDs, private links, unpublished art, or other held source bodies into this public repository.

**SOURCE != PROJECTION**  
**ARCHIVE != BOOK**  
**COPY != NEW OCCURRENCE**  
**DESCENDANT != ANCESTOR**

A title may be registered while its source body remains held elsewhere.

## States

- `manuscript` — substantial authored text exists.
- `active-book` — actively developing as a book.
- `proto-book` — book-shaped idea or chapter cluster not yet consolidated.
- `in-world-book` — fictional publication that may also become a real publication.
- `source-corpus` — material feeding a book without being the book.
- `fossil` — older artifact preserved before final classification.
- `publication` — a release/production lineage exists.
- `story-world` — canon exists, but a standalone manuscript may not.

## Release states

- `held` — registered in the house; source body remains private/unreleased.
- `production-active` — a public lemonPRESS production lane already exists.
- `candidate` — may later cross into physical/digital/crawler/archive production.
- `unresolved` — identity or autonomy still needs source archaeology.
