# ARCHIVE PRESS

This branch preserves the house's publication memory.

It is not an editorial brain and not a replacement for source repositories or authoring workspaces.

## Scope

- admitted source snapshots
- publication receipts
- release lineage
- checksums and identifiers
- source-to-edition relations
- supersession records
- withdrawn or failed editions
- reconstruction notes

## Laws

> **ARCHIVE != VERDICT**

> **PRESERVED != CANONIZED**

> **MISSING != NONEXISTENT**

> **DESCENDANT != ANCESTOR**

The archive exists so later editions can know what they descended from without rewriting the birthday of the source.
