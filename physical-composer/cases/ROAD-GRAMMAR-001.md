# ROAD GRAMMAR 001 — Failure Becomes Instrument

## Trigger

Press Run 001 fed **THE ROAD DREW ITSELF** into Physical Composer 001.

The result exposed a weakness: the composer could recognize ordinary codex, booklet, staircase, workbook, pamphlet, folio, and mechanical-book pressures, but it did not know how to hear:

- road;
- traversal;
- found material;
- source receipts;
- companion geometry.

Falling back to a codex was lawful, but insufficiently imaginative.

That failure becomes a regression specimen.

## Mutation

Three reusable physical grammars are added.

### Route / Traversal Book

Triggered by road, route, path, trail, journey, sequence, traversal, walk, or stations.

It asks whether progression itself can become physical navigation.

Potential moves include:
- station-like section openings;
- cumulative route markers;
- an inspectable source road;
- lay-flat or accordion behavior when page count and vendor permit.

### Trace / Receipt Edition

Triggered by found, source, receipt, trace, archive, provenance, quoted, or citation.

It asks how provenance can remain physically inspectable without turning the book into footnote sludge.

Potential moves include:
- source labels as navigation furniture;
- visible distinction between quoted source and connective tissue;
- rear source road or foldout receipt;
- explicit marking of descendant production matter.

### Companion Pair Geometry

Triggered by companion, paired, pair, partner, or paired_with.

It treats the relation between books as a physical design surface while preserving independent edition identity.

Potential moves include:
- removable band or sleeve;
- inverse or mirrored edge behavior;
- opposed orientation;
- relation cues that disappear cleanly when the books are separated.

## Hard refusals

    SAME TRIM != SAME WORK
    COMPANION != DEPENDENT
    SOURCE RECEIPT != READING CLUTTER
    ROAD METAPHOR != PHYSICAL NAVIGATION
    RECOMMENDATION != SELECTION

## Regression expectation

For cases/road-drew-itself.json the composer must include:

- route-book
- trace-edition
- companion-geometry

Ordinary codex remains available as a control.

The mutation is successful when the failing specimen broadens the instrument without becoming a hard-coded exception.
