# PHYSICAL COMPOSER — INTERFACE 001

**State:** interface seam only. No autonomous selection authority.

The Physical Composer sits before Press Gate.

Its job is to imagine what a work could become when paper, scale, sequence, binding, handling, absence, inserts, folds, writable surfaces, translucency, edge behavior, and other material constraints become compositional instruments.

Its job is **not** to authorize a production choice.

## Core distinction

    RECOMMENDATION != SELECTION
    FORM != SOURCE
    MATERIAL EFFECT != SOURCE FACT
    PROOF != PUBLICATION

## Input

A composer run may receive admitted work identity, selected source/upstream edition, intended reader encounter, budget/vendor constraints, accessibility constraints, fixed decisions, held decisions, and physical affordances worth exploring.

## Output

The composer emits a proposal with:

    INTENT
    FIXED
    RECOMMENDED
    HELD
    ALTERNATIVES
    MATERIAL MOVES
    RISKS
    ACCESSIBILITY
    VENDOR DEPENDENCIES
    EXPECTED LOSS
    HANDOFF

Every production decision carries one state:

    recommended
    human_selected
    held
    vendor_constrained
    rejected

Only human_selected or explicit external fixed constraints may become Press Gate production facts.

## Example material moves

The composer may propose saddle stitch, multiple pamphlets inside a band, narrower return leaves, gatefolds, translucent stock, removable decoder strips, intentional blank versos, writable receipt pages, cover-interior printing, gutter-dependent spreads, or edge marks visible only when the book is closed.

Each move should answer:

> **What can this physical carrier do that the digital ancestor cannot?**

## Handoff

    COMPOSER PROPOSAL
          |
          v
    HUMAN SELECTION
          |
          v
    SELECTED PHYSICAL DECISIONS
          |
          v
    PRESS GATE
          |
          v
    EDITION PACKET

Press Gate must not silently convert recommendations into selections.

> **The composer imagines the body. The gate proves which body crossed.**
