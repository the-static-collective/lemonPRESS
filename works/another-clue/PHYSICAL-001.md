# ANOTHER CLUE — PHYSICAL 001
## The Staircase Edition

**State:** physical proof design  
**Upstream:** admitted *Another Clue — Carrier Cut 001*

This arm does not ask how to print a digital file.

It asks what paper can do to the book's central trick.

## Material idea

The physical edition makes **rereading a tactile event**.

The first occurrences of recurring carriers are ordinary pages.

When the book returns to them later — THE KNIFE, AGAIN; THE EDGE, AGAIN; THE CUP, AGAIN; LITTLE YES, AGAIN — the return begins on a deliberately narrower **stair page**.

A stair page is a short-width leaf or visibly inset page block. The earlier source remains unchanged. What changes is the reader's hand position and the visible edge of the book.

The staircase literally comes later.

No explanatory graphic is required.

## Decoder strip

A removable bookmark / press strip carries only:

```text
SOURCE != PROJECTION
RETRIEVAL != COMPLETE READING
THE TRAIN MAY REVERSE.
THE TIMESTAMPS MAY NOT.
THE STAIRCASE CAME LATER.
```

It is not necessary to understand the book.

It is a tool the reader may discover, lose, lend, or place somewhere else.

## Print behavior

- body text stays restrained;
- source labels are typographic, not ornamental;
- connective tissue receives breathing room rather than a new typeface;
- repeated source carriers remain textually unchanged;
- the "AGAIN" sections get the material staircase treatment;
- the Source Road stays at the back as a receipt, not footnote clutter;
- one lemon mark may identify branch-local production matter, but never source matter.

## Held decisions

Trim size, paper stock, binding method, printer, color process, ISBN strategy, and exact stair-page fabrication remain open until a printer-specific proof is chosen.
