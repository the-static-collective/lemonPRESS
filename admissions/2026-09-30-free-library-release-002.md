## Non-destructive edition rule

`LP-NM-002` does not mutate the Release 001 copy of `LP-NM-001`.

## Carrier rule

The two nunuMath works were observed in paired PDF and DOCX carriers. Text extraction from those carriers is not identical. The archive therefore records paired carrier lineage without asserting byte identity or exact textual identity.
