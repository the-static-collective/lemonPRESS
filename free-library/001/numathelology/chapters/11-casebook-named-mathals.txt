CHAPTER 11
CASEBOOK OF NAMED MATHALS
11.1  SECTIONED-QUOTIENT-001
Definition 11.1. SECTIONED-QUOTIENT-001 is the composite x ↦ [x]ᴳ ↦ D_F([x]ᴳ), with carrier X, quotient grammar G, frame F, and decoder D recorded independently.
Use when a scalar or coordinate is produced from a richer equivalence class and later comparison risks confusing value equality with source identity.
11.2  RETURN-RECEIPT-001
Definition 11.2. RETURN-RECEIPT-001 is a return mathal whose selected endpoint closes while the retained receipt remains nontrivial.
Canonical mathematical neighbors include loops with nontrivial homotopy, pure braids, holonomy, and finite-state cycles with nonzero traversal count.
11.3  HOLD-FIBER-001
Definition 11.3. Given an observation map O, HOLD-FIBER-001 retains distinctions among elements of a fiber O⁻¹(y) until target-relative sufficiency has been established for the current question.
The mathal operationalizes the instruction not to quotient away a difference before proving it safe to forget.
11.4  DEFERRED-DISCRIMINATOR-001
Definition 11.4. When two worlds are currently observationally equivalent but retain distinguishable receipts, DEFERRED-DISCRIMINATOR-001 records the smallest known future observation that could separate them, or UNKNOWN if no such observation has been established.
11.5  RELATION-BIRTH-001
Definition 11.5. RELATION-BIRTH-001 maps a relation type to its minimum admissible arity together with a specimen-level formation receipt and any orientation signature.
11.6  CROSSING-SHEAR-001
Proposal 11.6. A crossing shear is a discrepancy produced when two paths that agree under one projection separate under another. The shear is indexed by the pair of decoders and the path family used.
Crossing shear is useful when “same result” claims depend strongly on which summary is active. It should not be promoted without a concrete pair of projections and a finite witness.
11.7  CARRIER-RUPTURE-001
Definition 11.7. CARRIER-RUPTURE-001 records a threshold at which a chosen relation class can no longer be represented in the existing carrier without violating a declared constraint.
K₅ relative to the simple planar carrier is the principal finite specimen in this edition.
11.8  SAME-STATE-DIFFERENT-WORLD-001
Definition 11.8. SSW-001 is a pair of distinct worlds with equal active observation and a target question that separates them.
The C₆ versus C₃⊔C₃ pair is the standard specimen. Its function is epistemic: it proves that re-expression of the available observation cannot restore an erased distinction.
11.9  ORIENTATION-SIGNATURE-001
Definition 11.9. An orientation signature is the minimum retained datum required to distinguish otherwise support-identical oriented structures under the chosen world.
For ordered bases, determinant sign is a canonical example.
11.10  FORMATION-MATHAL-10→01→2
The token 10→01→2 is treated in this edition as a formation mathal rather than an arithmetic identity. Its intended reading is multiplicity → distinction → relation: a mnemonic for a constitutive sequence in which two presences must first be distinguished before their relation can be typed. No claim is made that the glyph sequence is canonical.
Status 11.10. PROPOSAL / mnemonic formalism.
11.11  Use of the casebook
Named mathals are addresses. They exist to make a reusable crossing citeable without silently enlarging its scope. A casebook entry becomes a theorem only where a theorem is separately proved. Most entries in this chapter are definitions or proposed instruments.
Exercises
11.1. Choose one named mathal and produce a new finite specimen in an ordinary mathematical domain.
11.2. Construct a case where HOLD-FIBER-001 is unnecessary because target-relative sufficiency has been proved.
11.3. Give a candidate crossing shear and identify the two decoders explicitly.
11.4. Invent a named mathal only if it compresses a repeatable operation. State the exact non-collapse it preserves.
