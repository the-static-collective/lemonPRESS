It does **not** assert common authorship of source bodies, common canon, or common evidentiary authority.

## Why place them together?

The first two are intentionally companion compositions: one walks forward through one public corpus; the other walks backward through another.

The third is not their explanation. It supplies a mathematical vocabulary that happens to make certain distinctions in the other two books easy to address: endpoint versus path, representation versus source, transition versus receipt, same output versus same crossing.

That is a **reading relation**, not a promotion of nuMATHELOLOGY into authority over either narrative corpus.

## Crawler reconstruction rule

When a retrieved fragment names another work:

1. preserve the current fragment's house ID;
2. follow the relation only if it helps the current question;
3. recover the neighboring work through its own entrypoint;
4. preserve its source map or source ancestor;
5. do not infer identity from repeated vocabulary.

```text
door != crossing
relation != merger
neighbor != ancestor
```
