# UNFINISHED BUSINESS — Manga Seed 001
## CASE ZERO: THE END THAT WOULD NOT ARRIVE

**Status:** Unadmitted creative proposal, not published, not assigned a house ID. This is an original supernatural action/mystery manga treatment. Its conceptual relation to GHoT's UNFINISHED BUSINESS 001 is thematic only; fiction creates no runtime requirement or evidence.

**Premise:** Every unfinished thing leaves a remainder. Most become harmless sediment. A few learn how to knock. The city's midnight Bureau of Last Chances investigates when a remainder gains enough shape to enter the world. Its most dangerous policy is simple: **finish everything.**

**Series law:** UNFINISHED ≠ FAILED. RELEASE ≠ ERASURE. FINISHING ≠ SAVING.

### Characters / visual grammar
- **NERI VALE (19)** — bicycle courier, patched cloak over ordinary work clothes; stubborn and economical with words. She can hear the final beat of music that has never been written.
- **TALLOW** — a clerk with a lantern for a head and carbon-paper sleeves. He stamps cases, never souls. Possibly the last honest bureaucrat in the dead city.
- **THE FORECLOSER** — silhouette assembled from crossed-out task lists; it seals possible futures by forcing endings that were never chosen.
- **THE UNFINISHED CHOIR** — murmuring forms that emerge when unrelated abandoned things start harmonizing.

**Graphic approach:** High-contrast ink, spirited 90s supernatural action pacing, abrupt comedy-to-horror transitions, negative space that turns into architecture; no use of existing franchise characters or designs. Pages alternate dense bureaucratic marginalia with explosive silent action. A torn page can become a gateway, but paper alone confers no permission.

## Chapter 0 — Six-page opening storyboard

### PAGE 1 — No final beat (5 panels)

**1. Establishing, long horizontal:** A city before sunrise. An elevated train cuts across a violet-black skyline. Billboards flash "FINISH WHAT YOU START."

**SFX:** KA-THUNK / KA-THUNK / KA—  

**2. Extreme close-up:** A cassette spinning in Neri's backpack. A label in thick marker: *SIDE B — DO NOT THROW AWAY*.

**3. Medium:** Neri brakes her bicycle. The train overhead has stopped between two stations. Every window contains the same man, standing in precisely the same position.

**NERI (thought):** That isn't an echo.

**4. Inset:** Her wristwatch reads 5:59. Its second hand runs backward.

**5. Full-bleed bottom:** The missing last beat of the cassette appears in the sky as an enormous white space. A silhouette begins pulling itself out of it.

**CAPTION:** Not everything unfinished is dead.

### PAGE 2 — A very small government (6 panels)

**1. Medium:** A lantern-headed figure stands beside Neri's parked bicycle, carrying a thin folder.

**TALLOW:** Please do not touch the apparition. It is awaiting classification.

**2. Tight:** Neri eyes the folder. It reads *CASE 0 — TRAIN THAT NEVER ARRIVED*.

**NERI:** You classify trains now?

**3. Tiny comedy panel:** Tallow clicks an enormous stamp. It produces no ink.

**TALLOW:** Only the ones which have been arriving for thirty-seven years.

**4. Wide:** The train opens its doors onto empty air. A hundred pairs of shoes step out, but no bodies follow.

**5. Close:** Neri hears something behind the rhythm: one soft, unfinished note from the cassette.

**NERI:** It's not trying to arrive.

**6. Snap zoom:** Tallow's lantern flames blue.

**TALLOW:** Then someone has filed the wrong question.

### PAGE 3 — Finish everything (4 panels)

**1. Page-dominant splash:** The FORECLOSER unfolds from the train's timetable, a giant made of checkboxes and ticket stubs. Its hands are staplers as long as telephone poles.

**FORECLOSER:** ALL DEPARTURES MUST TERMINATE.

**2. Small:** A stamp slams onto the train's front windshield: *COMPLETED*.

**3. Violent action:** The train begins folding like a paper model, passengers and all. Neri leaps onto its roof.

**NERI:** You didn't ask anybody!

**4. Black panel, thin white letters:** A voice from inside the carriage.

**VOICE:** We were never trying to get there.

### PAGE 4 — The first wrong ending (5 panels)

**1. Long narrow:** Neri runs the length of the train as bridges and stations flash past her, none staying put.

**2. Full close-up:** She tears the cassette open, exposing its spooled tape.

**NERI:** I can hear where the beat stops.

**3. Strange quiet:** The riders appear for the first time: not ghosts, but people waiting inside versions of the day they never lived.

**4. Inside carriage:** One old conductor holds a punched ticket. Its destination is blank.

**CONDUCTOR:** Thirty-seven years ago, we chose to stay and bring the last passenger home.

**5. Neri freezes.**

**NERI:** So the unfinished trip wasn't a mistake.

### PAGE 5 — A case is not a command (5 panels)

**1. Smash:** The Forecloser drives its stapler through the roof. Neri catches it with the bicycle chain looped around her wrist.

**2. Close:** Tallow, still on the street far below, opens the folder to a blank line titled *DISPOSITION*.

**TALLOW:** Completion is only one of the available dispositions!

**3. Page-wide:** Neri pulls the handwritten timetable free. From behind it spills an impossible platform: an old woman carrying flowers, a child with a birthday cake, the unnamed last passenger.

**4. Eye close-up:** Neri stares at the unfinished ticket. She does not fill in its destination.

**NERI:** The journey was never ours to finish.

**5. Silent large:** She unfastens the chain. The train unfolds instead of collapsing. Its passengers choose whether to step into daylight.

### PAGE 6 — Released, not erased (5 panels)

**1. Morning, calm:** The train passes the last station without stopping. Its timetable remains blank.

**2. Tight:** Tallow stamps the folder: *RELEASED*. Below it in smaller type: *RECORD RETAINED*.

**3. Two-shot:** Neri and Tallow by the bicycle.

**NERI:** Was that your job? To tell the truth about what happened?

**TALLOW:** My job is to ensure the case does not rewrite the people inside it.

**4. Tiny comedy:** He hands her a badge: *TEMPORARY INVESTIGATOR*. It's printed on a bus transfer, already expiring.

**NERI:** This doesn't even get me a train ride.

**TALLOW:** Most appointments don't.

**5. Final full-width:** Deep underground, shelves upon shelves of unopened files. Their tabs begin glowing one after another, like an orchestra tuning.

**SFX:** TIK. TIK. TIK. TIK.

**CAPTION:** There are nine million open cases.

**SMALL VOICE, OFF-PANEL:** Find the one that's yours.

**END CHAPTER ZERO.**

## Series engine and boundaries

**Case loop:** particular event → apparent haunting → contradictory witness → forced premature ending → distinct possible dispositions → consequence → record that cannot be silently rewritten.

A case may end in **COMPLETED**, **CONTINUED**, **RELEASED**, **REFUSED**, or **UNRESOLVED**. None is a moral ranking. A released case remains a narrated occurrence; unresolved cases may return with changed evidence, never automatic authority.

**Season arc:** The Forecloser is not a monster that simply loves paperwork. It was created because the Bureau was drowning in promises it could not redeem. It has learned to confuse *closure of records* with *closure of lives*. The central conflict is whether the city can tolerate genuine remainder.

**Creative relation:** The software experiment explores unfinished work. This manga explores unfinished lives. They can inform one another through cited, human-approved adaptation, but the fiction does not become machine policy and machine status does not dictate story canon.
