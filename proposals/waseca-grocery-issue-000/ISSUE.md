# THE GROCERY THAT ISN'T HERE YET
**lemonPRESS / Waseca Issue 000 / experimental proof / October 25, 2026**

> Good. We can still ask what it should become.

A community-owned grocery is being explored in Waseca, Minnesota. That possibility belongs in a conversation wider than any one social-media feed.

Before anyone can hand us a shopping basket, we can hand one another a question:

**What would make a grocery store genuinely worth building and owning together?**

Here is a remarkably small experiment. Ask one question, invite one neighbor whose answer may differ from yours, and carry back one useful thing learned—with permission.

## Four roads into the same conversation

**AFFORDABLE** — What everyday staples, prices, and tradeoffs would make ordinary shopping possible?

**LOCAL** — What would make participation feasible for nearby growers and makers, without guessing at their prices, schedules or willingness?

**ACCESS** — What transportation, hours, navigation, and accessibility questions should the future store answer?

**BELONGING** — How might everyday residents shape the idea without first becoming professional volunteers or joining another committee?

The answers may disagree. That is useful. Nobody has to promise labor, money, or membership to answer.

## The invitation that travels

Bring somebody a copy of this sheet or one of the four cut-apart cards. You can print it at home, leave it where independent flyers are allowed, or share the interactive page through an ordinary personal social-media account.

From there people may examine the question, compose their own voluntary invitation, or explicitly opt to send a public-safe idea to The Static Collective / Abundent for private review. Ideas do **not** become official Waseca Area Food Cooperative registrations, dues, volunteer assignments, endorsements, verified completions, or members.

We cannot yet point to a verified online cooperative enrollment link, price, or authoritative member count. Interest is not enrollment. When a genuine official enrollment process becomes verifiable, its details belong to the cooperative, not to this independent page.

## A press that changes carriers

The same **exact** work can have a web reading copy, a crawler-readable text edition, a one-sheet printable broadsheet, and four hand-to-hand invitation cards. Those are distinct carriers. Printing a page does not enroll its recipient. Scanning a QR code does not give permission to republish their response.

The next issue must earn its contents: separately reviewed public-safe answers, consented quotations, actual corrections, and independently confirmed work. A hypothetical reply is not an interview. A crowd is not a confirmed membership roll. A proposal is not a decision.

A paper sheet can outlast a social post. A genuine neighborhood conversation can outlast both.
