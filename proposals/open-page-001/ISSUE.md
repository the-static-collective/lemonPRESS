# THE OPEN PAGE — lemonPRESS Issue 001
## Somebody out there knows how.

**Independent experimental newspaper dated October 25, 2026.** Published as a public preview in response to the idea of widening the first Waseca grocery paper into an open notice board for practical expertise throughout the Coalition of Care ecosystem and neighboring projects.

### The editorial mechanism

Every open call has an identifier, a bounded useful question, an example first action, a status label and a boundary. The newspaper publishes **types of expertise**, not identities of people in need or sensitive underlying case details.

An actual reader can voluntarily respond to **The Static Collective / Abundent**, which owns this independent press. They are not automatically applying for membership in the Waseca Area Food Cooperative, becoming part of the Coalition of Care, pledging work, or accepting employment. Individual organizational participation requires separate direct consent.

**Eight current calls:** cooperative operations; safe cross-organization handoffs; food distribution; environmental stewardship; arts gathering; accessible websites; community press and printing; shared neighborhood/congregational tables.

**Source-status fidelity:** Public reporting on October 22 said the grocery organizers were interested in co-op and local food expertise (verify whether still current). Coalition of Care's own charter and contemporaneous October 24 reporting support its stated network-weaving aims but do not establish these proposed classifieds as its official recruiting needs. The remaining notices are independently drafted calls, including our own requests for press and web-testing volunteers.

### Two directions

- **I have something useful to offer:** Select listing `LP-OC-###`, copy the private-response starter, open `https://abundent.org/contact/?via=lemonpress-open-page`, include the ID and only the contact details you choose. We do not relay contact information to a third party without consent.
- **I know a need:** Use `https://abundent.org/contact/?via=lemonpress-open-need` to privately propose a notice. Do not disclose personal cases, names, health details, addresses, contact info for others or an organization's unconfirmed problem. Before publication, editor must classify as reported lead, independently owned request or exploratory proposal; verify owner permission for any claim presented as an actual institutional request.

If responses are received, editorial return can publish only aggregate or separately consented reviewed outcomes; a self-report is not an independent confirmation. Criticism, refusals and corrections are welcome.

### Honest newspaper sections

1. **This Week:** A sourced, dated Waseca co-op report and the Coalition's public charter/reporting. Neither source is treated as a contract or endorsement of the paper.
2. **Open Calls:** The eight notices, filterable by category, with precise role types and small asks.
3. **The Reply Desk:** Voluntary private response or propose an anonymized call.
4. **Corrections and Returns:** Update claims when stale and publish only meaningful, appropriately permissioned evidence.
