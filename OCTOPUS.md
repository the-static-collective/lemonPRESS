# OCTOPUS HOUSE TOPOLOGY

lemonPRESS uses long-lived production branches as arms of one publishing body.

```text
                         main
                   HOUSE / CATALOG
                         |
                    ADMITTED WORK
                         |
        +----------------+----------------+
        |                |                |
 press/physical     press/digital    press/crawler
        \                |                /
         \               |               /
                    press/archive
```

This is not a rank diagram.

It is a specialization diagram.

## Arm rule

Each arm should become **more itself**, not a weak copy of another arm.

### physical

Ask:

> What can paper, binding, sequence, scale, handling, absence, and material surprise do that a screen cannot?

### digital

Ask:

> What can navigation, accessibility, search, linking, responsive layout, and alternate reading routes do without turning the work into a database?

### crawler

Ask:

> What survives when the first encounter is partial, out of order, cached, quoted, embedded, summarized, or retrieved by a machine?

### archive

Ask:

> What must remain fixed enough that all later descendants can still tell where they came from?

## Shared WRENCH law

> **DO NOT ONLY EXPAND THE PLOT. CHANGE THE MEDIUM.**

A successful crossing leaves the next arm a usable difference, not an obligation to imitate it.
