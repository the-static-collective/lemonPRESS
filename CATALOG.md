### What Release 001 means

A book can be on this shelf without every production surface being identical.

```text
source ancestor
      ↓
house admission
      ↓
physical / digital / crawler / archive descendants
```

**SOURCE != PROJECTION** remains in force. A crawler text is not the typeset book. A print proof is not the source document. A source map is not a substitute for the cited corpus.

### The three-book geometry

Release 001 is deliberately not a trilogy.

- **The Road Drew Itself** composes a forward reading road through *The Daily Slice*.
- **¿another clue?** composes a backward companion road through *National Treasure*.
- **nuMATHELOLOGY** is an independent reference work about declared worlds, relations, crossings, receipts, loss, and structural transition.

The shelf may expose relations among them without inventing a shared source authority.
