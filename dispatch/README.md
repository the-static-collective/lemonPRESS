**Status: EDITORIAL PROTOTYPE / HOLD — not released, distributed, sent, or endorsed.**
Date opened: 2026-10-26. First service area: Waseca–Greater Mankato, Minnesota.

**News for what happens next.** An independent, locally grounded newspaper with one consented editorial issue and three distinct distributions: a no-account web edition, an opt-in email digest, and an accessible print edition. The physical paper can also serve as an optional, **free-to-read** PENNY participation demonstration.

## Why this exists
Use published, correctable community reporting to make constructive local opportunities legible. Begin with the emerging Waseca grocery conversation, Coalition of Care context, and a small, consent-based local practical-help story. A possible story is not a verified event. A source lead is not a partner. A person's interest is not consent to publish them.

## One source, three editions
Retain one immutable, issue-scoped editorial source, with source references, review, publication consent, correction log, and distinct render receipts. Print / web / newsletter outputs are derivative editions, not independent authorities. Publication requires an editor's explicit release decision. No automatic import from private Workboard, survivor care systems, internal contact notes, or field observations.

## Minimum publication and safety protocol
- Obtain permission before identifying meeting attendees, reproducing personal statements, or naming a partner as participating.
- Quote only verified words, attribute only with consent. Never import survivor case material.
- Separate OPINION / PROPOSAL / MEETING RECOLLECTION / SOURCED FACT / HUMAN-CONFIRMED RESULT.
- Display correction contact, issue version, and what changed. Public removal requests go to a human editor. Digital withdrawal cannot erase copies already printed.
- Support a no-device participation pathway; no phone/app/penny required to obtain the paper.
- Sponsorship and print support must be labeled, kept independent from editorial choices, and accounted for separately from coin gifts.
- Do not make automatic community matches or claim service delivery from a posted need.
