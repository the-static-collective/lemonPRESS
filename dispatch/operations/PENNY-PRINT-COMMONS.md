# PENNY PRESS circulation covenant — proposal only

**Free paper first.** A copy may always be taken without payment, coin, registration, contact, QR scan, explanation or performance. The phrase “ONE PENNY / EVERYBODY WELCOME” is a symbolic invitation, not a claim of an established token economy.

## Pilot physical ritual
Place a small bowl of **real U.S. pennies** beside a clearly distinguished collection box. Visitors may optionally take a penny and drop it in the box. They can also contribute one of their own pennies or skip the ritual altogether.

- Treat all coins as **physical funds**, not backed, transferable, spendable crypto tokens or credits. No currency is minted or redeemed.
- Box ownership, custodianship, safeguarding, site permission and allocation of proceeds must be documented before collecting money.
- Require two people for every public claim that a box was physically counted; retain separate observations; cash counts are not independent proof of a financial transaction.
- Publish only aggregated, properly reconciled totals and expense receipts. No subscriber or participant tracking through pennies.
- Print support and advertisements need a public policy that prevents sponsors from buying editorial control.
- Cancelable test: if the box is removed, the paper stays free.

## What PENNY-014 does *not* authorize
Jubilee-treasury PENNY-014 is a **synthetic experimental proof** of held allocation, simulated physical custody, backed release, and redemption. It is not deployed, backed by confirmed physical cash, or authorized for monetary claims. This paper uses no PENNY-014 issuance. PLEDGE ≠ CASH RECEIPT; OBSERVATION ≠ INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION.
